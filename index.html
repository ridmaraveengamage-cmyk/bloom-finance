Excel / PDF statement workflow

  1. For Excel (.xlsx), open the statement in Excel and choose Save As → CSV UTF-8, then import the CSV above.
  2. For PDF statements, use your bank's CSV/Excel export when available. PDF layouts differ and automatic extraction can misread amounts, dates or debit/credit columns.
  3. Compare imported totals with your original statement before relying on analytics.
