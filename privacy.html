Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Bloom Finance is a personal finance app. This page explains what information it uses and why.

What we collect

When you create an account we store your email address, username, mobile number and country. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, our authentication and database provider.

Your financial data

The accounts, transactions, budgets, goals and other financial details you enter are currently saved in your own web browser on your device, kept separately for each account. They are not sent to our servers. If you clear your browser data, they are removed, so please use Backup in the app.

How we use information

Only to sign you in, run the app and keep your account secure. We do not sell your information, show advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers needed to run Bloom (Supabase for sign-in, Google if you choose Google sign-in, and GitHub Pages for hosting the website).

Groups

If you join a group, you choose what to share with it. Nothing is shared by default.

Your choices

You can update your details in the app. To delete your account and the information we hold, contact us and we will remove it.
